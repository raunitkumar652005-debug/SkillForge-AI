# SkillForge AI

> **Learn. Build. Practice. Get Hired.**
> *Forge Your Skills. Shape Your Future.*

SkillForge AI is an AI-powered developer learning platform for mastering full-stack concepts, building real-world projects, practicing AI-graded quizzes, preparing with realistic mock technical interviews, and tracking continuous learning progress.

---

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic

- **Midnight Navy** (`#0B1020`): Primary background surface
- **Slate Glass** (`#151D32`): Elevating glassmorphism cards and interactive panels
- **Electric Violet** (`#9185FF`): Primary brand accent, navigation highlights, and actions
- **Neon Teal** (`#38D9C5`): Success metrics, progress indicators, and AI synthesis accents
- **Soft White** (`#F4F6FC`): High-contrast readable typography on dark surfaces

---

## Core Capabilities

1. **Structured Full-Stack Curriculum**: 31 sequential modules spanning fundamentals through modern architecture.
2. **AI Mentor Terminal**: Conversational coding assistant providing real-time guidance and code reviews.
3. **Interactive Quiz Mode**: Descriptive answers graded with tailored AI feedback alongside objective multiple-choice drills.
4. **Mock Technical Interviews**: Adaptive interview simulations assessing knowledge and behavioral delivery.
5. **Applied Project Portfolio**: Production-grade projects reinforcing theoretical concepts.
6. **Unified Analytics**: Granular progress tracking, activity logs, and milestone completion metrics.
